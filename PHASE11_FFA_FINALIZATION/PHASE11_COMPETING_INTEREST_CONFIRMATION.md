# Phase 11 / Task C3 — Declaration of competing interests

**Status: RESEARCHER CONFIRMATION REQUIRED. NOTHING HAS BEEN DECLARED.**

## 1. What FFA/Elsevier requires (verified 2026-11-10)

* Authors must disclose **any** financial and personal relationships with other
  people or organisations that could inappropriately influence or bias their
  work.
* The declarations tool at **`declarations.elsevier.com`** "should always be
  completed" and the generated statement **uploaded at the submission step**.
* The following interest types are to be considered: employment, consultancy,
  stock ownership, honoraria, paid expert testimony, patent applications or
  registrations, and grants or other funding.
* The interests of **all** authors must be declared. Elsevier's guidance is
  that the corresponding author collects the declarations from co-authors.
* The declaration is also normally printed in the article.

## 2. Why this cannot be completed by the agent

Whether an author holds a competing interest is a **personal fact** known only
to that author. No such fact can be inferred from the manuscript, the
repository, or any document in this project. Asserting "no competing
interests" on the authors' behalf would be a **substantive declaration made
without authority**, and if false it is a matter of research integrity.
