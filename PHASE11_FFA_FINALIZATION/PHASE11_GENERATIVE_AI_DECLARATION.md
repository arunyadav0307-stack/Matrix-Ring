# Phase 11 / Task C5 — Generative AI declaration

**Status: RESEARCHER CONFIRMATION REQUIRED — the declaration is MANDATORY and
the exact wording is for the authors to settle.**

## 1. What FFA/Elsevier requires (verified 2026-11-10)

* Elsevier requires authors to **declare the use of generative AI and
  AI-assisted technologies in the manuscript preparation process**.
* The declaration must be placed in a **new section before the reference
  list**, with the section title

  > Declaration of generative AI and AI-assisted technologies in the
  > manuscript preparation process

* The requirement covers **all** generative-AI and AI-assisted use in
  preparation, not only writing.
* The published exemption is narrow: the requirement **does not** apply to
  basic tools for checking grammar, spelling and references.
* Elsevier's policy permits generative AI to create **explanatory images and
  data visualisations**, but **not** to alter or fabricate primary data; such
  use must be disclosed **both** in the relevant figure caption **and** in the
  general declaration statement.
* FFA's guide points to the Elsevier-wide generative-AI policies page
  (`elsevier.com/about/policies-and-standards/generative-ai-policies-for-journals`),
  so journal-specific wording beyond the section title could not be verified
  and is recorded as **UNVERIFIED**.

## 2. Why a declaration is required here, and why it cannot be evaded

**AI assistance was used extensively in the preparation of this manuscript.**
The exemption for grammar, spelling and reference checking plainly does not
cover the work described below. A declaration is therefore mandatory, and
submitting without one would be a misrepresentation.

This note is deliberate and is not optional. It would be easy — and wrong — to
omit AI assistance merely because it was delivered through conversational
prompts rather than a named writing product.

## 3. What must be disclosed — the factual basis

The following describes the AI assistance that this project received. **The
authors must verify this description and supply the tool names and dates,
which are not recorded here because they are not reliably known to the
agent.**

| area | nature of AI assistance |
|---|---|
| Manuscript drafting | Large portions of the LaTeX source were drafted and revised by an AI agent operating in this repository. |
| Proof structure and exposition | Theorem statements, proof outlines and section organisation were developed with AI assistance. |
| Computational artefacts | Scripts generating the parameter tables, certification data and supplementary material were written with AI assistance. |
| Verification and audit | The Phase 8, 8A, 8B, 9, 10 and 11 audit, verification and compliance documents were produced by an AI agent. |
| Abstract revision | The Phase 11 abstract revision (Task B) was drafted by an AI agent and is disclosed as such. |
| Reference checking | Bibliographic cross-checking was performed by an AI agent — **note: this goes beyond the narrow grammar/spelling/reference exemption, because it included substantive venue attribution work.** |

**Not AI-generated:** the mathematical results themselves originated with the
researcher; all computed parameters were produced by deterministic scripts
whose outputs are reproducible from the `PAPER/supplementary/` package; and no
generative AI was used to alter, fabricate or synthesise primary data. The
manuscript contains **0 figures**, so the figure-caption disclosure requirement
does not arise.
