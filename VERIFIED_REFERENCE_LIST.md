# Verified reference list

**Verification standard.** Bibliographic identity, venue/year and stated scope were checked using publisher pages, arXiv records, proceedings listings or the supplied sources. For paywalled papers, this verifies metadata/abstract and cited scope—not every theorem or proof. The search is targeted, not exhaustive. The 2025 ISIT record's DOI was not confirmed. No item below is evidence that the exact revised ring has or lacks prior art unless explicitly stated.

## A. Foundational quantum-code construction

**[R1]** A. R. Calderbank, E. M. Rains, P. W. Shor, and N. J. A. Sloane, “Quantum error correction via codes over GF(4),” *IEEE Transactions on Information Theory* 44(4), 1369–1387 (1998). DOI: [10.1109/18.681315](https://doi.org/10.1109/18.681315). Standard field-level quantum-code foundation.

**[R2]** J. A. Wood, “Duality for modules over finite rings and applications to coding theory,” *American Journal of Mathematics* 121(3), 555–575 (1999). DOI: [10.1353/ajm.1999.0024](https://doi.org/10.1353/ajm.1999.0024). Frobenius-ring/module duality background.

## B. Ring and constacyclic-code structure

**[R3]** C. A. Castillo-Guillén, C. Rentería-Márquez, and H. Tapia-Recillas, “Constacyclic codes over finite local Frobenius non-chain rings with nilpotency index 3,” *Finite Fields and Their Applications* 43, 1–21 (2017). DOI: [10.1016/j.ffa.2016.08.004](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.ffa.2016.08.004). Covers specific low-nilpotency local Frobenius families, not the revised ring's full parameter family.

**[R4]** C. A. Castillo-Guillén, C. Rentería-Márquez, and H. Tapia-Recillas, “Duals of constacyclic codes over finite local Frobenius non-chain rings of length 4,” *Discrete Mathematics* 341 (2018), 919–933. Publisher record: [ScienceDirect](https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/pii/S0012365X17304302). Relevant duality precedent for a bounded family of local Frobenius non-chain rings.

**[R5]** S. T. Dougherty, A. Kaya, and E. Saltürk, “Cyclic codes over local Frobenius rings of order 16,” *Advances in Mathematics of Communications* 11(1), 99–114 (2017). DOI: [10.3934/amc.2017005](https://doi.org/10.3934/amc.2017005). Ring-code and Gray-map background; not the revised \(\mathcal R_k\) family in general.

## C. Constacyclic-to-quantum prior art

**[R6]** A. Alahmadi, H. Islam, O. Prakash, P. Solé, A. Alkenani, N. Muthana, and R. Hijazi, “New quantum codes from constacyclic codes over a non-chain ring,” *Quantum Information Processing* 20, article 60 (2021). DOI: [10.1007/s11128-020-02977-y](https://doi.org/10.1007/s11128-020-02977-y). Direct precedent for non-chain constacyclic codes and quantum constructions.

**[R7]** H. Islam, S. Patel, and O. Prakash, “A family of constacyclic codes over a class of non-chain rings \(A_{q,r}\) and new quantum codes,” *Journal of Applied Mathematics and Computing* 68, 2493–2514 (2022; published online 2021). DOI: [10.1007/s12190-021-01623-9](https://doi.org/10.1007/s12190-021-01623-9). Abstract states Hermitian dual-containing criteria for constacyclic and skew-constacyclic codes over its distinct ring family.

**[R8]** S. Bhardwaj, M. Goyal, and M. Raka, “New Quantum Codes from Constacyclic Codes over a General Non-Chain Ring,” arXiv:2212.02821 (submitted 6 December 2022). [arXiv record](https://arxiv.org/abs/2212.02821). Studies a general family built from polynomials splitting into distinct linear factors. Cite as a preprint unless publication status is independently updated.

**[R9]** S. Biswas and M. Bhaintwal, “New quantum codes from self-orthogonal cyclic codes over \(\mathbb F_{q^2}[u]/\langle u^k\rangle\),” *Quantum Information Processing* 20, article 303 (2021). DOI: [10.1007/s11128-021-03230-w](https://doi.org/10.1007/s11128-021-03230-w). Direct chain-ring cyclic/Gray/quantum precedent.

**[R10]** Y. Wang, X. Kai, Z. Sun, and S. Zhu, “Quantum codes from Hermitian dual-containing constacyclic codes over \(\mathbb F_{q^2}+v\mathbb F_{q^2}\),” *Quantum Information Processing* 20, article 122 (2021). DOI: [10.1007/s11128-021-03052-w](https://doi.org/10.1007/s11128-021-03052-w). Uses a different non-chain ring and a duality-preserving Gray map.

**[R11]** Y. Wang, X. Kai, Z. Sun, and S. Zhu, “Hermitian dual-containing constacyclic codes over \(\mathbb F_{q^2}+v_1\mathbb F_{q^2}+\cdots+v_r\mathbb F_{q^2}\) and new quantum codes,” *Cryptography and Communications* 15(1), 145–158 (2023). DOI: [10.1007/s12095-022-00593-4](https://doi.org/10.1007/s12095-022-00593-4). Adjacent non-chain-ring Gray/quantum work.

**[R12]** Z. Tian, J. Gao, and Y. Gao, “Hulls of constacyclic codes over finite non-chain rings and their applications in quantum codes construction,” *Quantum Information Processing* 23, article 9 (2024). DOI: [10.1007/s11128-023-04230-8](https://doi.org/10.1007/s11128-023-04230-8). Publisher abstract describes Euclidean/Hermitian hulls and quantum constructions for \(\mathbb F_q+v\mathbb F_q\), \(v^2=v\).

**[R13]** Y. Tang, H. Xu, T. Yao, and X. Kai, “New quantum codes from constacyclic codes over finite chain rings,” *Quantum Information Processing* 23, article 311 (2024). DOI: [10.1007/s11128-024-04519-2](https://doi.org/10.1007/s11128-024-04519-2). Uses a chain ring with one nilpotent variable and Hermitian/trace constructions.

**[R14]** Y. Tang, H. Xu, T. Yao, and X. Kai, “Quantum codes from constacyclic codes over finite non-chain rings,” *Quantum Information Processing* 25, article 305 (2026), published 3 September 2026. DOI: [10.1007/s11128-026-05334-7](https://doi.org/10.1007/s11128-026-05334-7). Current adjacent prior art over a different finite non-chain family; the ring is not \(\mathcal R_k\).

**[R15]** B. Kong and X. Zheng, “Non-binary quantum codes from constacyclic codes over \(\mathbb F_q[u_1,\ldots,u_k]/\langle u_i^3-u_i, u_i u_j-u_j u_i\rangle\),” *Open Mathematics* 20(1), 1013–1020 (2022). DOI: [10.1515/math-2022-0459](https://doi.org/10.1515/math-2022-0459). Another multi-variable commutative non-chain constacyclic/quantum precedent; its defining polynomials differ from the nilpotent complete intersection proposed here.

## D. Prior art on the abandoned matrix-ring direction

**[R16]** S. Patel, O. Prakash, and H. Islam, “Cyclic codes over \(M_4(\mathbb F_2+u\mathbb F_2)\),” *Cryptography and Communications* 14, 1021–1034 (2022). DOI: [10.1007/s12095-022-00572-9](https://doi.org/10.1007/s12095-022-00572-9). The publisher abstract describes a noncommutative non-chain matrix ring and an \(\mathbb F_{16}\)-coordinate presentation. A published correction is [10.1007/s12095-022-00584-5](https://doi.org/10.1007/s12095-022-00584-5). The full 2022 article was not line-by-line audited in this reconstruction.

**[R17]** S. R. Joshi, S. Patel, and O. Prakash, “Construction of new codes over \(\mathbb F_{16}\) from cyclic codes over \(M_4(\mathbb F_2+u\mathbb F_2+u^2\mathbb F_2)\),” IEEE International Symposium on Information Theory (ISIT 2025), proceedings listing; cited as Ref. 19 in [R18]. The title/scope and proceedings citation were confirmed in the supplied/source materials; DOI was not confirmed.

**[R18]** S. R. Joshi, S. Patel, and O. Prakash, “Construction of Cyclic Codes over a Class of Matrix Rings,” arXiv:2602.18255v1, submitted 20 February 2026. [arXiv record](https://arxiv.org/abs/2602.18255). Direct cyclic-code/dual/Gray-map work for a matrix-ring family; preprint status as checked.

**[R19]** S. R. Joshi, S. Patel, and O. Prakash, “Quantum codes construction from cyclic codes over matrix rings,” *Quantum Information Processing* 25, article 207 (2026), published 9 June 2026. DOI: [10.1007/s11128-026-05230-0](https://doi.org/10.1007/s11128-026-05230-0). Its matrix setting is \(M_3\), so it is adjacent, not a direct result for \(\mathcal R_k\).

## E. Additional adjacent non-chain constacyclic/quantum work

**[R20]** F. Ma, J. Gao, and F.-W. Fu, “New non-binary quantum codes from constacyclic codes over \(\mathbb F_q[u,v]/\langle u^2-1,v^2-v,uv-vu\rangle\),” *Advances in Mathematics of Communications* 13(3), 421–434 (2019). DOI: [10.3934/amc.2019027](https://doi.org/10.3934/amc.2019027). This is another published non-chain-ring constacyclic-to-quantum construction over a ring distinct from \(\mathcal R_k\).
