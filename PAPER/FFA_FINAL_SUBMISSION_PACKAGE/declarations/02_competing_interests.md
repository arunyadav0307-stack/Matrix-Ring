# Declaration of competing interests

**STATUS: CONFIRMED by the researcher on 2026-11-10.**

## Confirmed declaration text

> The authors declare that they have no known competing financial interests or
> personal relationships that could have appeared to influence the work
> reported in this paper.

This text is **already inserted** in the compiled manuscript, in the section
*Declaration of competing interests*, placed before the reference list as
Elsevier requires.

## How this was confirmed

The researcher was asked directly and selected "Nothing to declare" from the
available options. **It was not assumed.** The register does not treat the
absence of a competing interest as a default.

## Official Elsevier requirement (verified 2026-11-10)

> "All authors must disclose any financial and personal relationships with
> other people or organizations that could inappropriately influence or bias
> their work. Examples of potential competing interests include: Employment,
> Consultancies, Stock ownership, Honoraria, Paid expert testimony, Patent
> applications or registrations, Grants or any other funding, Affiliation with
> the journal as an editor or advisory board member."
>
> "The declarations tool should **always** be completed."
>
> "Authors with no competing interests to declare should select the option
> *'I have nothing to declare.'*"
>
> "The resulting Word document containing your declaration should be uploaded
> at the 'attach/upload files' step in the submission process. Save the
> document in .doc/.docx format. Author signatures are not required."

## Outstanding procedural action

**The wording is settled, but the submission step is not.** The researcher
must:

1. Complete the declarations tool at `declarations.elsevier.com`.
2. Select "I have nothing to declare."
3. Download the generated Word document.
4. **Upload it** at the "attach/upload files" step during submission.

The agent cannot perform any of these steps.

## If circumstances change

If any author acquires a competing interest before submission, this
confirmation is void and the declaration must be replaced with the specific
disclosure. Note that **funding is disclosed separately** (see
`03_funding.md`) and a grant does not need to be repeated here as a competing
interest, though Elsevier lists "Grants or any other funding" among the
examples to consider.
