# Funding declaration

**STATUS: CONFIRMED by the researcher on 2026-11-10.**

## Confirmed declaration text

> This research did not receive any specific grant from funding agencies in the
> public, commercial, or not-for-profit sectors.

This text is **already inserted** in the compiled manuscript, in the section
*Funding*, placed before the reference list.

## How this was confirmed

The researcher was asked directly and selected "No specific grant". **It was
not inferred** from the absence of a grant record in the repository. Absence
of evidence is not evidence of absence: institutional support, scholarships,
departmental allocations and in-kind contributions are all funding and none
would appear in this repository. The status was therefore explicitly
confirmed rather than assumed.

## Official Elsevier requirement (verified 2026-11-10)

> "Authors must disclose any funding sources that provided financial support
> for the conduct of the research and/or preparation of the article. The role
> of sponsors, if any, should be declared in relation to the study design,
> collection, analysis, and interpretation of data, writing of the report, and
> decision to submit the article for publication. If funding sources had no
> such involvement, this should be stated in your submission."
>
> "If no funding has been provided for the research, please include the
> following sentence: *This research did not receive any specific grant from
> funding agencies in the public, commercial, or not-for-profit sectors.*"

## The alternative that was NOT used

Preserved here so the choice is auditable:

> Funding: This work was supported by [FULL OFFICIAL NAME OF FUNDER],
> [COUNTRY] [grant number [NUMBER]].

This alternative applies only if the researcher later identifies a funder. In
that case the sponsor's role must also be stated.

## Interaction with the APC

The FFA article publishing charge is **USD 3 240** excluding taxes for gold
open access (verified 2026-11-10). **Subscription publication carries no
charge**, so no funding decision is required for that route. If gold OA is
chosen, the APC must be paid from some source — which would not retroactively
make this work "funded" for declaration purposes, but should be considered
separately.
