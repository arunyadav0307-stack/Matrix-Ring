\begin{abstract}
We construct $q$-ary quantum codes from the Galois hulls of cyclic codes over
the noncommutative matrix ring $R=M_2(A_t)$,
$A_t=\F_q[u]/\langle u^t\rangle$; codes are right ideals of
$R_n=R[X]/\langle X^n-1\rangle$.  Duality is driven by the anti-automorphism
$\theta_s=\sigma_s\circ(\cdot)^{\mathsf T}$, $\sigma_s$ the coefficient
Frobenius.  We prove the duality and hull theory for arbitrary right ideals,
with no principality hypothesis, and refute the conjecture that Galois hulls
are principal: hulls with minimal generator number $\MU_B=3$ exist, and
$\MU_B\ge3$ forces $t\ge2$ and $p\mid n$.  The coefficient-expansion map to
$\F_q^{4tn}$ transports the Galois pairing to an explicit
$\widehat\sigma_s$-Hermitian form with Hankel Gram matrix
$I_{4n}\otimes H_t$ --- not the Euclidean form unless $t=1$ --- and preserves
duality and hulls, including the non-principal ones.  Every Galois hull is
self-orthogonal for the transported form, with no reflexivity hypothesis.
Three quantum routes of length $N=4tn$ follow: a twisted stabilizer route, a
Euclidean/CSS route, and an entanglement-assisted route
$[[N,N-k-h_E,d;k-h_E]]_q$.  We show that the distance is the minimum weight of
the quotient $D^{\perp_E}/\Hull_E(D)$, and derive a coset Singleton bound
$K+d\le N+1$ not implied by the entanglement-assisted Singleton bound.
Exhaustive computation certifies $28$ codes, each by two independent
methods: one exact, and one quantum search exact for $11$ codes and a
lower bound for $17$, among them this framework's first distance-$4$
stabilizer codes, $[[24,8,4]]_9$ and $[[20,4,4]]_4$, and entanglement-assisted
codes up to $[[20,2,10;18]]_4$.  No record parameters are claimed.

\smallskip
\noindent\textbf{Keywords.} cyclic codes over rings; matrix rings; Galois
duality; Galois hulls; Gray map; quantum stabilizer codes;
entanglement-assisted quantum codes.

\smallskip
\noindent\textbf{MSC 2020.} 94B05, 94B15, 94B60, 16S50, 11T71.
\end{abstract}
