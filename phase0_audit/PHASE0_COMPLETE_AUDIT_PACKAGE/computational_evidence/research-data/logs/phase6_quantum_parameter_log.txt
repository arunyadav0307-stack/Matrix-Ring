E1.  the statement, the proof, and what is verified
---------------------------------------------------
  Claim (COMPUTATIONALLY VERIFIED ONLY / PROVED for the linear part).
  Let p = 2 and t even, and let H_t be the transported Hankel form
  matrix (Phase 4, T-177: H_t is ALTERNATING).  Then for EVERY
  invertible F_q-matrix S the matrix S^T H_t S is again alternating,
  because (Sx)^T H_t (Sx) = 0 for all x.  Since I_t is not
  alternating (e_i^T I_t e_i = 1), no congruence S^T H_t S = I_t
  exists.  The obstruction therefore applies to EVERY linear
  physical-coordinate transformation, not merely to the block form
  I_{4n} (x) T of the Phase-5 remark.  (In characteristic 2 a
  symmetric matrix M is alternating iff M has zero diagonal, since
  x^T M x = sum_i M_ii x_i^2 and x -> x^2 is a bijection of F_q.)

   (q,t)   | H_t alternating | S^T H_t S alternating (200 random S) | I_t alternating
  ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
  (4,2)   | True           | True (200 invertible S)                  | False
  (4,4)   | True           | True (200 invertible S)                  | False
  (8,2)   | True           | True (200 invertible S)                  | False
  (8,4)   | True           | True (200 invertible S)                  | False
  (4,6)   | True           | True (200 invertible S)                  | False
  [EXH] E1  for p = 2 and t even, H_t is alternating and alternatingness is PRESERVED BY EVERY congruence S^T H_t S (so no linear change of physical coordinates maps H_t to I_t) PASS  [SMP] 200 random invertible S per (q,t), 5 parameter pairs

E2.  the exact domain of validity (t ODD is different)
------------------------------------------------------
  PROVED.  Let p = 2 and t = 2m+1 be ODD.  The transported form is
  B(x,y) = sum_{i+j=t-1} x_i y_j = <x, rho y>, rho the reversal
  permutation.  Split the index set into the fixed point {m} and
  the m pairs {i, t-1-i}: on each pair the Gram matrix is
  [[0,1],[1,0]] (a hyperbolic plane), on the fixed point it is [1],
  and the summands are mutually orthogonal.  Hence
       H_t  ~  (hyperbolic plane)^{(+) m}  (+)  <1>.
  For one such plane (basis u,v with B(u,u)=B(v,v)=0, B(u,v)=1)
  together with z, B(z,z)=1, z orthogonal to u and v, the vectors
       a = z+u,   b = z+v,   c = z+u+v
  satisfy B(a,a)=B(b,b)=B(c,c)=1 and B(a,b)=B(a,c)=B(b,c)=0, so
  <u,v,z> is isometric to I_3; absorbing the planes one at a time
  gives H_t ~ I_t for EVERY odd t.  A congruence therefore EXISTS
  for odd t, and the obstruction of E1 concerns t EVEN only.
  (Consistency: for t even the decomposition is m hyperbolic planes
  with no central <1>, and B(x,x) = 0 identically on a hyperbolic
  plane, so no orthonormal basis exists -- exactly E1.)
  NOTE.  A naive 'diagonalise by the greedy pivot and rescale' is
  NOT sufficient: the restriction of B to e^{perp} can be
  alternating for one admissible pivot and non-alternating for
  another, so the construction below backtracks over pivots.

  (q,t)=(4,1): H_t alternating = False ; a congruence S with S^T H_t S = I_t is CONSTRUCTED and verified
  (q,t)=(4,3): H_t alternating = False ; a congruence S with S^T H_t S = I_t is CONSTRUCTED and verified
  [EXH] E2  for t ODD a congruence S^T H_t S = I_t is constructed explicitly (symmetric elimination + Frobenius scaling) and verified, so the obstruction is specific to t even PASS  [EXH] constructive, verified by direct multiplication

  (q,t)=(4,5): H_t alternating = False ; a congruence S with S^T H_t S = I_t is CONSTRUCTED and verified
  (q,t)=(4,2): H_t alternating = True ; the constructive method returns no congruence (as it must: t even)
  (q,t)=(8,2): H_t alternating = True ; the constructive method returns no congruence (as it must: t even)

       SCOPE.  Nothing above says that good codes are impossible in
       characteristic two: it says that the particular route 'transform
       H_t into I_t and then use the standard symplectic form' is closed
       for p = 2, t even.  The routes R1, R2 and R3 of Phase 5 do NOT use
       that transformation -- they work with the Galois/Gram form directly
       -- and they produce codes over F_4 and F_8 (see below).  The
       failure of one coordinate transformation is therefore NOT a
       statement about the whole characteristic-two regime.
