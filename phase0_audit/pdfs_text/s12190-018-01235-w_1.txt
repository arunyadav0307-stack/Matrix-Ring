Joydeb Pal 1 · Sanjit Bhowmick 1 · Satya Bagchi 1
Received: 6 October 2018 / Published online: 3 January 2019

Abstract
In this article, keeping the huge research prospective of the study in mind, we consider
the non-commutative ring M4(F2),t h es e to fa l l4× 4 matrices over the ﬁeld F2 and
conﬁrm that this ring is isomorphic with the ring F16 + uF16 + u2F16 + u3F16, where
u4 = 0. Besides, we develop the structure of cyclic codes and their generators over
the ring. Also, making use of Gray map from M4(F2) to F4 16, we infer that the image
of a cyclic code is a linear code. Finally, our ﬁndings are authenticated by suitable
non-trivial examples.

1 Introduction
Cyclic codes over ﬁnite rings have been extensively studied due to their vast signif-
icance in the theory of error correcting codes. Besides, codes over ﬁnite rings have
experienced a remarkable growth after the development of linear codes over the ring
of integers modulo 4 obtained in [7]. In that article, the authors gave a new wing to the
topic by employing Gray map from Z4 to F2 2 which relates some illustrious non-linear
codes to some linear codes. Since then, codes have been thoroughly studied over ﬁnite
commutative rings by several mathematicians and consequently plenty of interesting
results on codes over ﬁnite commutative rings are there in the literature, among those
some of are related to chain [ 1,4,6,10] and some others are non-chain [ 13,14]. A very

3.1 Gray map
In [4], for the case of the ring F2 + uF2, the isometry map from a + ub to (b, a + b)
helps us to extend the Gray map Φ from A to F4 16 such that,
Φ(a + ub + u2c + u3d) ↦→ (d, c + d, b + c + d, a + b + c + d)
preserves Lee weight and Hamming weight.
Thus for any element x = a + ub + u2b+ u3b in A, extended Lee weight is deﬁned
as
wL(x) = wH (d) + wH (c + d) + wH (b + c + d) + wH (a + b + c + d),
where wL(·) and wH (·) stand for Lee weight and Hamming weight respectively. If
¯x = (x0, x1, x2,... xn−1) ∈ An , then Lee weight of ¯x is deﬁned as,
wL(¯x) = wL(x0) + wL(x1) + wL(x2) +···+ wL(xn−1).
Now if C is a linear code over A, then the Lee distance of the code is deﬁned by
dL = min{wL(¯x)|¯x ∈ C}.

4 Conclusion
In this paper, we have furnished cyclic codes over a non-commutative matrix ring
and exhibited that the right- A modules form a chain. Thus, the structure of the cyclic
codes over A follows from the structure of the cyclic codes over the ﬁnite chain rings.
Moreover, we have deﬁned a Gray map which outlines a correspondence between
M4(F2) to the ﬁeld of order 16. However, a purposeful research venture can be carried
forward on the self-dual codes over M4(F2). Further, the structure of the cyclic codes
over M3(F2) due to [ 9] are yet to be dealt with and can be thought of a challenging
open problem.
