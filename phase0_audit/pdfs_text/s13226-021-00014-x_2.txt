Abstract In this article, for a prime p such that p /C17 2o r3 ðmod 5 Þ, we identify cyclic codes of length N
over R ¼ M2ðFp þ uFpÞ, u2 ¼ 0 as right R-submodules of R½x/C138=hxN /C0 1i. Also, we deﬁne an isometry from
M2ðFp þ uFpÞ to Fp2 þ uFp2 þ vFp2 þ uvFp2 , where u2 ¼ v2 ¼ 0; uv ¼ vu and determine the structure of
cyclic codes, in particular self-dual cyclic codes of length N where gcd ðN; pÞ¼ 1. Moreover, several
optimal and near to optimal codes are obtained as the Gray images of these codes over R.

1 Introduction
The class of cyclic codes is an important class of linear codes which has been extensively studied over ﬁnite
rings after the establishing work of Hammons et al. [ 7] in 1994. Like commutative rings [ 9, 11–14],
noncommutative rings also play an important role in the development of error-correcting codes. Apart from
skew polynomial rings [ 4, 8, 10], rings of matrices have recently attracted a growing interest among
researchers. In 2012, Oggier et al. [ 16] considered the matrix ring M2ðF2Þ for space-time coding. In 2013,
Alahmadi et al. [ 1] studied cyclic codes over M2ðF2Þ and explored certain self-dual F4-codes. Also, in 2014,
Falcunit and Sison [ 5] investigated the structure of cyclic codes over M2ðFpÞ by using an isometry between
M2ðFpÞ and Fp2 þ uFp2 . Recently, Luo and Udaya [ 15] considered the matrix ring M2ðF2 þ uF2Þ and
obtained several optimal F4-codes of length 16 and 20, respectively as the Gray images of cyclic codes.
Further, Pal et al. [ 17] and Bhowmick et al. [ 2] have studied cyclic codes over M4ðF2Þ and self-dual cyclic
codes over M2ðZ4Þ, respectively.
In this paper, for any integer k /C21 0, we consider the matrix ring M2ðFp þ uFpÞ where u2 ¼ 0 and p is a
prime of the form p ¼ 5k þ 2o r p ¼ 5k þ 3 and investigate the structure of cyclic codes over M2ðFp þ uFpÞ.

6 Conclusion
In this article, we studied cyclic, and in particular self-dual cyclic codes over R ¼ M2ðFp þ uFpÞ.W e
obtained necessary and sufﬁcient conditions for cyclic codes to be self-dual over R. It is proved that cyclic
codes of length N with gcd ðN; pÞ¼ 1 are principally generated. As an application, we presented some
optimal and good codes of length 16 and 20 over F9, respectively. We believe that some more new and
optimal codes of different length can be obtained by considering their Gray images in our set up.
