## 2. The map and target form — proof versus computation

**VERIFIED UNDER CONDITIONS — G01/G02.** The map expands each matrix entry in the basis \(1,u,\ldots,u^{t-1}\), with a fixed ordering:
\[
\varphi_n:R^n\longrightarrow\mathbb F_q^N,\qquad N=tr^2n.
\]
It is a bijective \(\mathbb F_q\)-linear coordinate expansion. It is not a map to a field space with an automatically given \(R\)-module structure. A transported regular action can be defined separately.

Let \(H_t\) have ones on its antidiagonal and zeros elsewhere. The top coefficient of a product of two truncated entries pairs \(u^a\) with \(u^{t-1-a}\), while \(\operatorname{Tr}(\theta_k(X)Y)\) pairs the same matrix-entry positions. Consequently
\[
b_k(x,y)=\sigma_k(\varphi_n(x))\,J\,\varphi_n(y)^T,
\qquad J=I_{r^2n}\otimes H_t.
\]
This is the substantive coordinate calculation, not a conclusion inferred from the check count. \(H_t^2=I\), hence \(J\) is invertible. G T-176/T-177 give the map and Gram calculation for matrix size 2; the same entrywise calculation explains the general \(r\) formula. The independent audit directly recomputed matrix multiplication/top trace at \(q=4,t=2\), using all 64 basis pairs and 500 seeded pairs for each \(k=0,1\), without assuming the displayed Gram matrix.

**VERIFIED UNDER CONDITIONS — G03/G04.** For a **right \(R\)-module** \(C\), the scalar/ring orthogonal equality established in the pairing audit gives
\[
\varphi_n(C^{\perp_k})=\varphi_n(C)^{\perp_{k,J}},\qquad
\varphi_n(\operatorname{Hull}_k(C))=
\varphi_n(C)\cap\varphi_n(C)^{\perp_{k,J}}.
\]
Sources: G T-178/T-179 and M `thm:grayduality`/`thm:grayhull`. The first equality uses the **specific transported pairing**. The second uses injectivity and preservation of intersections. Neither requires the hull to be principal. Neither says that an arbitrary Gray map preserves orthogonality.

The double dual in these coordinates remains \(\sigma_{2k}\), not an unconditional identity. A hull is self-orthogonal for the transported form even outside the globally reflexive regime; this does not make that form Euclidean or turn a semilinear graph stabilizer into an \(\mathbb F_q\)-linear one.

## 3. Metrics and quasi-cyclicity

**VERIFIED UNDER CONDITIONS — G05/G07.** For matrix-size \(r\),
\[
w_{ring-H}\le w_{entry}\le w_{coef}\le t\,w_{entry},\qquad
w_{ring-H}\le\operatorname{wt}_H\varphi_n(x)\le tr^2 w_{ring-H}.
\]
The expansion is an isometry for the **coefficient** metric, not the ordinary ring-symbol Hamming metric. The original matrix-size-2 bounds use \(4t\). Non-monomial coordinate changes can alter Hamming weights and require recomputation of distances.

A ring cyclic shift becomes a field shift by \(tr^2\) coordinates. Thus the image is quasi-cyclic of **index \(tr^2\)** and co-index \(n\), not generally cyclic. At \(r=2\) the index is \(4t\); at proposed \(r=3\), it would be **\(9t\)**, not 3. G T-183 is the correct size-2 statement. Changing matrix size alone is not a new theorem or a novelty justification.

**VERIFIED UNDER CONDITIONS — G06.** G T-181/T-182's integrality obstruction applies to the **stated normalization** of homogeneous weight. For \(M_2(\mathbb F_q)\), its nonzero values
\(1+1/(q^2-1)\) and \(1-1/((q-1)^2(q+1))\) are nonintegral, whereas Hamming weights are integers. This prevents an isometry into an ordinary Hamming space for that normalization. It does **not** settle all rescaled weights or all possible metric constructions. The closed form uses an imported standard character-sum identity; it is not a new universal theorem certified by 463 tests.

## 4. Ordinary Euclidean, bilinear and Hermitian congruence are different

**VERIFIED UNDER CONDITIONS — G08.** The target form is literally Euclidean only when \(t=1\) and \(\sigma_k=id\). Otherwise CSS inclusion for the ordinary physical coordinates must be **checked**, or a proved transformation with its weight cost must be used. In particular, transported self-orthogonality is not ordinary Euclidean self-orthogonality by definition. The independent test `Gray_dual_not_standard_Euclidean` exhibits the distinction at \(uM_2(\mathbb F_4[u]/u^2)\).

**VERIFIED UNDER CONDITIONS — G09.** In the **bilinear** case \(\sigma_k=id\), characteristic two and even \(t\) make \(H_t\) alternating. Alternatingness is invariant under ordinary congruence, while \(I\) is not alternating. Thus no \(T^T H_t T=I\) exists. The canonical manuscript's definition of congruence explicitly assumes \(\sigma_k=id\); that restricted obstruction is sound.

The stronger readings require correction:

1. **INCORRECT — G10:** extending that obstruction to all Hermitian/sesquilinear twists. In \(\mathbb F_4\), using \(\alpha=2\), \(\alpha^2+\alpha+1=0\),
   \[
   T=\begin{pmatrix}1&1\\\alpha&\alpha+1\end{pmatrix},\quad
   \sigma_1(T)^T H_2T=I_2,
   \quad T^TH_2T=H_2.
   \]
   This independently checked counterexample does **not** give a monomial Hamming isometry, and does not refute the bilinear obstruction.
2. **INCORRECT — G11:** “zero diagonal means alternating” without characteristic/type qualifications. For the bilinear form over \(\mathbb F_9\), \((1,1)H_2(1,1)^T=2\ne0\). For the Hermitian form over \(\mathbb F_4\), the self-value at \((1,\alpha)\) is \(\alpha+\alpha^2=1\ne0\). M §06's general assertion that \(J\) is alternating iff \(t\) is even is too broad.
3. **INCORRECT — G12:** treating the displayed Phase-5 \(\mathbb F_9\) matrix \(T=\left(\begin{smallmatrix}3&2\\3&1\end{smallmatrix}\right)\) as a Hermitian congruence. Independent arithmetic finds \(T^TH_2T=I\) but \(\sigma_1(T)^TH_2T\ne I\). Its valid scope is bilinear.
4. **INCORRECT as a blanket consequence:** impossibility of an ambient form congruence does not prohibit every individual code from having a Euclidean/CSS inclusion. The original Gray log itself reports nonzero codes with Euclidean self-orthogonality at even \(t\). Direct ordinary-Euclidean EA construction also remains possible. Do not turn a statement about an ambient form into a prohibition on all code-specific routes.

See [independent_core_checks.json](evidence/independent_core_checks.json) and [critical_followup_checks.json](evidence/critical_followup_checks.json) for full matrices and values.

## 5. What the EXH/SMP labels do and do not certify

The rerun checks map properties, metrics, forms, image duals, hull intersections, shifts, congruences and negative controls. Full code-family enumeration is used for some small cases; larger cases are sampled. It is a mixed suite, not an exhaustive search of all parameters.

Two source-level qualifications matter even within these labels:

- At script line **982**, `C.r % gcd(C.r, 2*s) == 0` is a tautology. It prints an incorrect reflexivity header for nonreflexive cases. The subsequent C5 checks use the correct `((2*s) % C.r) == 0` at line **1026**, so this does **not** show the actual reflexivity assertions all passed on a tautology. Keep the defect's scope precise.
- The pairing-identity test at line **1002** uses `ws[:120] × ws[:120]`; the symmetry tests use `ws[:50] × ws[:50]`. Their label can inherit `EXH` from the ambient-word enumeration even though **not all pairs** are tested. A full ambient enumeration and an exhaustive Cartesian-pair test are different. The proof, not that label, supports the general identity.

G §0.2 also prints a scalar-form law with a matrix multiplier on the scalar value. An \(\mathbb F_q\)-valued form is \(\sigma_k\)-sesquilinear over **central field scalars**; multiplying its value by an arbitrary matrix is type-invalid. The ring-valued law belongs to \(B_k\), not to \(b_k\).

The review classifies “this exact 463-check log reproduces” as **VERIFIED**, a finite execution fact. Claim G13 classifies the suite's experimental support beyond literal execution as **COMPUTATIONALLY SUPPORTED ONLY**. Neither classification is a universal proof, and the count alone cannot validate Gray/quantum compatibility.

### Field encoding is a real reproducibility defect

The generator JSON documentation points to `matrix_ring_lib`, but the actual records use `ring_structure_checks.Field`. For integer little-endian polynomial coefficients, the latter uses

| Field | Actual records' modulus | Earlier `matrix_ring_lib` modulus |
|---|---|---|
| F4 | x²+x+1 | x²+x+1 |
| F8 | x³+x+1 | x³+x²+1 |
| F9 | x²+1 | x²+x+2 |

The fields are isomorphic, but their **integer labels are not interchangeable** without an explicit isomorphism. Using the documented older encoding creates dual-matrix mismatches in **5** of the **15** q=8/9 records checked under that wrong encoding. The independent recertification uses the actual records' encoding, stated in the script and result JSON. No original JSON or documentation was silently repaired.
