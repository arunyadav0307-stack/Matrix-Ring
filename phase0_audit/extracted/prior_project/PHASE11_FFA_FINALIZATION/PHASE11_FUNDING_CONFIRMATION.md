# Phase 11 / Task C4 — Funding declaration

**Status: RESEARCHER CONFIRMATION REQUIRED. NOTHING HAS BEEN DECLARED.**

## 1. What FFA/Elsevier requires (verified 2026-11-10)

* Authors must disclose **all funding sources** for the work.
* The **role of the sponsor** must be stated where the sponsor had any
  involvement in study design; collection, analysis and interpretation of
  data; writing of the report; or the decision to submit for publication.
* If the sponsor had **no such involvement**, that must be stated explicitly.
* If there is **no funding to declare**, Elsevier's standard wording is:

  > This research did not receive any specific grant from funding agencies in
  > the public, commercial, or not-for-profit sectors.

* Funders are identified by their **full official name**; grant numbers are
  given where they exist.

## 2. Why this cannot be completed by the agent

Two opposite errors are both available here, and both are serious:

* **Asserting "no funding"** from the mere absence of a grant number in the
  project files. Absence of evidence is not evidence of absence. Institutional
  support, a scholarship, a departmental allocation or an in-kind contribution
  are all funding and none would appear in this repository.
* **Inventing** a funder, agency or grant number.

The funding status of this work is therefore recorded as **UNCONFIRMED**, and
the staging copy presents both permitted outcomes without selecting one.
