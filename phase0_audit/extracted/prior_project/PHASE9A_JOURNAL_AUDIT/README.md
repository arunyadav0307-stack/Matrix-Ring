# PHASE9A_JOURNAL_AUDIT — superseded, retained for provenance only

**Status: SUPERSEDED. Do not use this directory as a source of facts.**

## Why this directory exists

Phase 9A was begun as a focused FFA-suitability audit and then **abandoned by
the researcher before completion**. Exactly one deliverable was produced:

* `PHASE9A_FFA_SUITABILITY_AUDIT.md` — a first-pass FFA suitability audit.

It is **not** a complete phase deliverable set. It was never approved, and its
scope was overtaken by Phase 10 (FFA official-guideline verification and
submission-readiness audit) and then by Phase 11 (finalization).

## Why it has not been deleted

Phase 11 Task F explicitly forbids deleting this directory. It is retained as
a provenance record of what was examined, at what date, and — importantly —
of a factual error that originated here and was later corrected.

## Known errors in `PHASE9A_FFA_SUITABILITY_AUDIT.md`

That document contains the claim that *"Two of the 13 cited works are FFA
papers: Fan–Zhang 2017 and Liu–Pan 2020."* **This is false.** Both are
*Designs, Codes and Cryptography* papers. The correct count of FFA papers
among the 13 cited works is **zero**. The document itself carries the
correction in its own correction table, but the surrounding narrative
predates the correction and must not be quoted.

**Do not cite venue attributions, journal counts or quartiles from this file.**
