# Final research title and abstract

## Title

**Hermitian Dual-Containing Constacyclic Codes over \(\mathbb F_{16}[U,V]/\langle U^k,V^4\rangle\) and 4-ary Quantum Codes**

### Scope note

The title intentionally replaces the original matrix-ring alphabet. The coefficient ring below is a commutative local Frobenius ring, not an alternative notation or isomorphic presentation of \(M_4(\mathbb F_2[u]/\langle u^k\rangle)\). It has the same cardinality, but its multiplication, center, radical quotient and module theory differ.

## Abstract

Let \(\mathcal R_k=\mathbb F_{16}[U,V]/\langle U^k,V^4\rangle\), with \(k\ge2\). The ring is a finite commutative local Frobenius ring with residue field \(\mathbb F_{16}\), socle \(\mathbb F_{16}U^{k-1}V^3\), and cardinality \(16^{4k}\). This project studies odd-length \(\lambda\)-constacyclic codes over \(\mathcal R_k\), where \(\lambda\in\mathbb F_{16}\) satisfies \(\lambda^5=1\). Coefficient conjugation \(a\mapsto a^4\), fixing \(U,V\), gives \(\overline\lambda^{-1}=\lambda\), and the square-free factorization of \(X^n-\lambda\) over \(\mathbb F_{16}\) yields a CRT decomposition into complete-intersection local components \(K_i[U,V]/\langle U^k,V^4\rangle\).

The proposed main result is a necessary-and-sufficient Hermitian dual-containment criterion for the explicitly restricted family of codes whose CRT components are monomial ideals. The criterion is to be stated in terms of the conjugate-reciprocal factor permutation and the monomial annihilator staircases. A top-socle functional defines a nondegenerate field-valued Hermitian form on \(\mathcal R_k^n\). The project will construct an \(\mathbb F_{16}\)-linear isometry to the standard Hermitian space \(\mathbb F_{16}^{4kn}\), prove exact dual transfer for \(\mathcal R_k\)-linear codes, and apply the Hermitian construction to obtain 4-ary quantum codes. Quantum dimensions follow from the verified field-code dimension; distances will be reported only when exactly computed or rigorously bounded.

Existing literature already contains constacyclic-to-quantum constructions over chain and non-chain rings, duality-preserving Gray maps, and direct cyclic-code work over the matrix-ring family that motivated the original proposal. Accordingly, the proposed contribution is restricted to the exact commutative ring, its monomial CRT dual criterion, and any independently verified quantum families. A targeted search did not identify an exact match, but the novelty claim remains conditional on a systematic literature review and proof of the proposed component duality.

**Keywords:** constacyclic codes; finite Frobenius rings; non-chain rings; Hermitian duality; monomial ideals; Gray maps; quantum stabilizer codes.
