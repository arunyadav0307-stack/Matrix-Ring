Is request ke current workspace mein `PHASE1_AUTHOR_DECISION_SHEET.md`, `PHASE1_HOLD_RESOLUTION_BRIEF.md` aur `PHASE1_BLOCKER_EVIDENCE_MAP.md` ki physical copies nahi mili. Source roles: decision sheet = recorded alternatives/recommendations; brief = unchanged scope, HOLD aur classification; evidence map = exact questions, evidence gaps, routes aur H-blockers. Koi naya literature verification is guide ka hissa nahi hai.

## 1. Guide ko kaise padhein

Har D-section mein ye alag cheezein hain:

1. **Unresolved question:** abhi kis baat ka answer/selection missing hai?
2. **Simple meaning:** wahi baat aasaan language mein; mathematics nahi badli gayi hai.
3. **Recorded choices — verbatim:** original completed decision sheet ka English choice-text, bina naya option jode.
4. **Choice-wise effect:** mathematical consequence, possible advantage, limitation aur risk. “Ho sakta hai” ko result/proof na samjhein.
5. **Arena recommendation:** sirf advisory. **Author decision: NOT RECORDED.**
6. **Phase-2 relevance:** kya input zaroori hai, aur us input se kya automatically clear nahi hota.

**Chaar alag layers:**

- **Recommendation:** Arena ka suggested route.
- **Author decision:** author ki explicit, recorded choice/factual response.
- **Verification:** actual source/version/hypothesis ya process evidence check hua ya nahi.
- **Proof obligation:** mathematical assertion sach hai aur uska valid argument hai ya nahi.

Inmein se koi ek doosre ka replacement nahi hai. Source mil jaana novelty prove nahi karta; author ka “haan” missing theorem ya invariant ko prove nahi karta. “Defer/unknown” honest recorded response ho sakta hai, lekin dependent blocker ko resolve nahi karta.

**RA / MU / AD / EV:** administrative documentation complete; mathematics unresolved; author decision required; external verification required. Ek item par multiple labels lag sakte hain. Specification likh dene se mathematical question RA nahi ban jaata.
