## S02 / NL03 — field MT Galois hull generator matrices

**Primary preprint technical text inspected:** https://arxiv.org/html/2305.15237v1 , chunks 0,12,13. Ramy F. Taki Eldin and Patrick Solé, *Generator polynomial matrices of the Galois hulls of multi-twisted codes*.

**Theorem 28 (preprint):** for a Λ-MT code over F_(p^e), 0≤κ<e and compatible shift constants Λ^(−p^(−κ))=Λ, the paper constructs a QC code Q_G from a polynomial matrix built from the input GPM G and reciprocal/Frobenius data. If H_G is a GPM of the κ-Galois dual of Q_G, then H_G G is a GPM of the hull. Both the theorem statement and its proof continuation were read; the proof uses the prior dimension formula and determinant-degree argument. This is positive existing generator-matrix coverage, not merely an abstract claim.
