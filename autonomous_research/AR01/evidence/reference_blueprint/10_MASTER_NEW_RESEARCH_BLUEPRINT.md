## 1. Title

**Generator Profiles of Galois Hulls of Principal Cyclic Codes over Matrix Rings.** The title is neutral about whether growth occurs. Stronger preservation/growth or quantum titles require actual results and later author approval.

## 2. Motivation

MS03 and the audit already establish a framework in which cyclic right ideals correspond to embedded modules and hulls are intersections with a specified dual module. In a non-chain local factor, a submodule of a two-generated module can require more generators; the special relationship to a dual may impose additional restrictions. Explaining that restriction or growth mechanism is the proposed mathematical motivation, not merely constructing more codes.

## 3. Verified literature baseline

Published anti-isomorphism/Frobenius duality, field/commutative-ring Galois hulls, repeated-root scalar hulls, MT/QC hull generator matrices, Artinian μ/type/dual invariants and skew/Galois combinations are occupied terrain. The bounded fresh review adds or sharpens NL01–NL11; the inherited source/cell ledgers and A8 matrix-ring Gray/CSS predecessor remain binding. [02](02_LITERATURE_AND_NOVELTY_POSITIONING.md) separates primary-record/preview/selected-text evidence from complete proof applicability.

No “first matrix-ring quantum”, “first q8 EA”, generic hull-generator, or ingredient-only skew×Galois claim is available. Several primary technical comparisons remain necessary. A11 metadata, A5a author order and the MT2025/2026 version relationship remain qualified; missing metadata is not evidence of nonexistence.

## 4. Precise proposed gap

**Internal unresolved question:** can a principal cyclic code have a nonprincipal Galois hull in the repeated-root nilpotent setting (MS03 O-3.1b)? Known nonprincipal hull witnesses start from nonprincipal codes. The inherited q4,t2,n2 census does not settle the question over other fields or multiplicities.

**Proposed differentiated gap:** a structural generator-profile/principality criterion for that principal-input subclass, retaining the embedding and induced-form data that coarse dimensions forget.

**External status: X — NOT CERTIFIED UNOCCUPIED.** Scalar classifications, GPM/intersection methods or Artinian algebra may already imply part or all of it. Phase1 must test those reductions before proof/implementation effort. Internal openness does not establish external priority.

## 5. Primary objective

Seek a nonroutine theorem/classification explaining the attainable generator profiles of H_s(W) for μ_B(W)≤2, and hence principal-hull preservation versus generator growth. Initial proposed class: q=2^m,m≥2; r=2,t=2,n=2; all s. Any extension to e=p^a>2, multiple factors or r≥3 is conditional, not an automatic parameter enlargement.

## 6. Research questions and conjecture posture

Core questions RQ1–RQ8 in [03](03_SELECTED_RESEARCH_PROBLEM.md) ask: existence/preservation, sufficient embedding/profile data, sharp spectrum, twist dependence, global coupling and structural certificates. RQ9 is optional physical relevance.

**PC1** is an explicitly exploratory principal-hull-preservation conjecture in the smallest proposed local family. It is neither adopted nor proved; only the old q4 evidence supports that instance. Arena recommends question-only posture until the gap check. A counterexample, a preservation theorem, an already-known result or a no-go outcome are all possible. No later result may assume PC1 or old H-3A.1/H-3A.2.

## 7. Algebra and standing assumptions

q=p^m; A_t=F_q[u]/(u^t); R=M_r(A_t), primarily r=2; X central; B=A_t[X]/(X^n−1); S≅M_r(B); **right ideals**; first-slot θ_s=σ_s∘transpose with u,X fixed; rev is coefficient reversal. Field degree m and matrix size r are not interchangeable.

Known positive DD is +σ_(2s)(C), not unconditional reflexivity. Product-of-chain-ring generation is not freeness. The side, slot, trace level and exact induced form are part of every statement. These are inherited inputs, not the proposed new contribution.

## 8. Definitions and framework

For the column Morita module W, I_W={M:im M⊆W}; principal iff μ_B(W)≤r. Define D_s(W) as the module of the **actual global dual**, H_s(W)=W∩D_s(W), and record generator growth Δ_s(W) and principality margin μ_B(H_s(W))−r. Local factors use a source-checked model K[u,w]/(u^t,w^e) and induced maps/forms, not an invented standard dot product.

These definitions are research bookkeeping, not claimed novel invariants. A normal form must transport the pairing and retain embedding information; arbitrary ambient GL operations are not automatically isometries. Details and non-waivable exclusions are in [04](04_MATHEMATICAL_FRAMEWORK.md).

## 11. Computational methodology

Design only: separately specified field/coordinate contracts; definition-based and module-structural hull paths compared as **subspaces**; residue/radical minimal-generator certificates; independent arithmetic; explicit family/sample/timeout labels; negative controls for sides, reversal, forms and representation lengths.

Proposed future pilot cap: ≤3 families, ≤2,000 new distinct inputs/family, total2 CPU-hours/4GiB/250MiB, subject to explicit approval and preflight. No scientific scripts are imported or executed now. Old finite campaigns are reused as evidence rather than gratuitously rerun. [06](06_COMPUTATIONAL_METHODOLOGY.md) contains the method, not an implementation.

## 12. Quantum strategy, only if justified

Default: algebra-first; quantum optional. Coefficient expansion transports to the specified J/s form, not automatically Euclidean. Standard Euclidean EA uses **h_E**, c=k−h_E, K=N−k−h_E and a valid Bob completion, not μ_B(H_s). SO/DC/R1 have different stabilizer inputs/orthogonals/logical-error sets. General trace/additive labels require Fp-level treatment.

No distance, record, low-entanglement ceiling or “first q8” claim is made. If no new metric-qualified physical consequence survives QG1–QG6, omit the quantum track. [07](07_QUANTUM_CONSTRUCTION_STRATEGY.md) supplies its separate gates.
