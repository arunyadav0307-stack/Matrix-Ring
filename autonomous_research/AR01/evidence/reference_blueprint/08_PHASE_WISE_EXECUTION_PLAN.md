## 3. Detailed work packages and exit criteria

### Phase0 — blueprint delivery (authorized now)

**Permitted:** source/report/register study, bounded literature retrieval, comparison, proposal writing, file integrity tooling and ZIP packaging.  
**Forbidden:** new proofs, scientific implementations/imports/experiments, new examples, old corrections, manuscript edits/builds, commits/pushes or editorial action.

**Measurable exit:** all ten requested narratives exist; all18 topics map to substantive sections; P1/P2/P3 compared with weighted scoring; every pending new author decision has alternatives, recommendation, mathematical justification and consequences; all43 audit families and16 RF findings remain linked; original preservation and ZIP/hash checks pass. These are **delivery criteria**, not mathematical GO.

### Phase1 — first future phase: exact novelty and scope gate

**Entry:** NAD01 and NAD18 explicit approval; exact old-source/submission facts requested under NAD02; provisional class/twist boundaries agreed. If the author cannot yet identify a later source, that uncertainty remains visible and blocks affected overlap claims.

**Work:** inspect exact primary theorem statements and dependencies of MS03, foundational anti-isomorphism/form literature, NL01–NL06, NL10–NL11 and their relevant cited predecessors. Resolve the MT2025/2026 version relationship if relied on. Record access failures, not invented content. Search by related module/intersection/syzygy terminology, not only “matrix Galois hull”. No theorem proving or scientific implementation in this phase.

**Required artifacts:** exact-gap comparison ledger; primary-source/version/bibliography ledger; old/new contribution boundary; scoped question/target revision; unresolved-access/metadata register; author-decision update.

**Measurable exit:**

1. At least12 relevant theorem/source comparison rows, covering MS03, the decisive algebraic foundations and NL01/NL02/NL03/NL04/NL05/NL06/NL10/NL11; a count alone is insufficient.
2. Each proposed core claim NT03/NT04/NT05 has at least one direct primary-theorem comparison, or remains explicitly X/blocked.
3. No load-bearing positive gap conclusion depends solely on an abstract's silence.
4. Explain whether the candidate is a routine specialization of scalar hull classification, GPM/intersection generators or Artinian duality bounds.
5. Author confirms the research class, twist scope, acceptable negative outcomes and independent-review route.

**Outcome:** GO to a **separately approved Phase2**, NARROW and return for approval, or STOP if no substantive unoccupied problem remains. This is a reasoned bounded novelty assessment, not proof of all-literature absence.

### Phase2 — foundations and faithful local specification

**Entry:** Phase1 review accepted and Phase2 explicitly authorized.  
**Work:** NT01 inherited-lemma applicability ledger; NT02 local/global coordinate and form contract; precise principal-input class and allowed equivalences. Review existing proofs, without editing the old paper. If genuinely new proof work is needed beyond this specification, request a scope amendment rather than smuggle it into source review.

**Measurable exit:** every imported lemma lists side/slot/scalar field/quantifiers and valid proof location; all local maps have domains/codomains/inverses where claimed; input/hull representations and factor coupling are unambiguous; no freeness, automatic Hermitian, Euclidean or physical assumption remains hidden. The first pilot's finite cases and failure controls are specified before code.

**Outcome:** approve or reject the exact research contract; then stop for Phase3/4 authority.

### Phase3 — bounded pilot and falsification (not currently authorized)

**Entry:** explicit approval of implementation and the selected cases/resource envelope under NAD10/NAD14.  
**Work:** construct a **new** disposable research implementation according to [06](06_COMPUTATIONAL_METHODOLOGY.md), not a repair of old scripts. Reuse sealed old evidence as reference; compare named objects via actual matrices. Run a bounded pilot on approved classes, with independent arithmetic and negative controls.

**Proposed cap:** ≤3 families, ≤2,000 distinct new inputs/family unless a smaller complete class is proved, total2 CPU-hours/4GiB/250MiB artifacts. Not permission to spend these resources now.

**Measurable exit:** every visited object has field/coordinate metadata, closure, actual hull subspace and local μ certificate; two calculation paths agree as subspaces or flag discrepancies; every control's nonvacuous scope is recorded; sample/exhaustive/timeout/distance statuses distinct; no old input changed. A search without counterexamples leaves PC1 unproved.

**Outcome:** choose preservation, growth or re-scope as a **research strategy**, not a mathematical verdict; obtain separate theory approval.

### Phase4 — core structural theory

**Entry:** approved exact NT03/NT04 statements/classes, informed but not proved by the pilot.  
**Work:** seek complete structural and principality/growth proofs, allowing restriction, refutation or failure. No result is demanded to be positive.

**Measurable exit:** every claimed theorem has a complete proof and dependency list; the claimed class/normal form has a coverage argument; the criterion is explicitly necessary/sufficient or only one-sided; proposed invariants are not claimed sufficient without proof. A growth example has μ(input)≤2 and μ(hull)>2. A preservation theorem states parameter/twist restrictions. External comparison is updated at the **final** statement level.

**Outcome:** if only an old theorem or a routine corollary remains, stop/re-scope; no forced paper. Independent proof feedback precedes extension.

### Phase5 — sharpness and structural extension

**Entry:** accepted core results and author-selected NT05/NT06/NT07 scope.  
**Work:** determine a sharp spectrum/bound/preservation boundary, explain twist/embedding dependence, and only then consider e>2 or multiple factors. Matrix-rank3 is not an automatic next step.

**Measurable exit:** every extremum has an upper-bound proof and an attainment/exclusion argument in the retained class; global synthesis respects actual coupling; routine CRT and known max-μ facts are marked background. New examples have explicit source/matrix identity. Unsupported generalizations are withdrawn or remain open.

### Phase6 — independent computation and algorithm certificates

**Entry:** proof and dataset scope frozen for review.  
**Work:** independent implementation/verification of retained examples, generator minimality and NT08 if claimed; resource/time/completeness reporting. Distance work only for explicitly approved quantum objects.

**Measurable exit:**100% of retained new named examples have exact algebraic certificates; no unknown distance is printed as exact; every enumerated class has a completeness argument or a sample label; no source flags masquerade as independent certificates; tables and theorem hypotheses agree. A verifier must catch at least the predeclared wrong-side/form/representation negative controls where meaningful.

### Phase7 — optional quantum bridge

**Entry:** NAD07 approval and a defensible proposed link to the core theorem.  
**Work:** NT09 under [07](07_QUANTUM_CONSTRUCTION_STRATEGY.md); separate physical fields, trace groups, phase/scalar-free lift or Bob completion, relative distance and resource comparisons.

**Measurable exit:** QG1–QG6 all satisfied for every retained route/claim, with independently certified named objects. Otherwise mark the track omitted/unresolved. Algebraic paper assessment proceeds independently; quantum failure cannot be concealed as a Gray-map assumption.

### Phase8 — independent new-paper audit (before drafting)

**Entry:** proposed final theorem/claim set, new example certificates and literature comparison frozen for review.  
**Work:** independent mathematical and computational review; proof-statement consistency; predecessor/own-work overlap; type/field/form/metric scope; optional physical validity; no priority based on finite counters.

**Measurable exit:**

- At least one nonroutine structural main theorem plus a meaningful proved consequence/classification survives, judged for substance rather than lemma count.
- Every retained universal claim is independently reviewed with no unresolved critical dependency; others are restricted, withdrawn or explicitly open.
- Every retained example has a named-object certificate, and all tables distinguish exact/lower-bound/sample status.
- Every novelty claim has source-specific theorem comparison; X/priority limits remain disclosed.
- Old-paper corrections remain a separate unimplemented HOLD unless separately authorized; no claim is presented as new merely because it repairs an old defect.

**Outcome:** mathematical/novelty GO for a *specific new-paper scope*, NARROW, or STOP. Integrity PASS alone never satisfies this gate.

### Phase9 — new manuscript, not a corrected MS03

**Entry:** Phase8 audit accepted and **explicit drafting approval**.  
**Work:** write a new manuscript structured around the surviving core theorem; cite old and external work, include truthful limitations, source-linked proofs and reproducible examples. Do not reuse the old abstract's broad chain as the main contribution.

**Measurable exit:** statement-to-proof-to-example-to-source traceability; independent text/math consistency check; author approval of title, contribution claims, source/PDF hashes, declarations and data statement. If assumptions or theorems change, return to Phase8. No manuscript is drafted in the present task.

### Phase10 — journal and publication decision

**Entry:** accepted new manuscript and current author/journal facts.  
**Work:** verify journal scope, current quartile in a named database/year/category if Q1 is required, submission policies, related-work disclosure, data/licence/AI declarations and actual old-paper editorial status.

**Measurable exit:** source-dated journal/metric/policy records; all authors approve; no concurrent/redundant-submission conflict; exact files/source hashes identified. **Submission, email, deposit, release, commit or push each needs explicit authority.** Preparation files are not evidence of a submission or acceptance.
