## 1. Status vocabulary and inherited boundary

- **E:** established/imported or inherited audited mathematics, conditional on exact hypotheses.
- **U:** unresolved inherited result/applicability question.
- **PC:** explicitly proposed conjecture, not a theorem or assumption.
- **RQ / TARGET:** a desired new question/result, not yet known to hold.
- **X:** externally unverified originality/priority or an incomplete comparison.
- **FINITE:** existing computational evidence, bounded by actual objects/range; not a universal theorem.

E01–E10 in [04](04_MATHEMATICAL_FRAMEWORK.md) are prerequisites. In particular the Morita hull formula, μ-to-principality threshold, CRT/max-μ identity, chain-regime generation, double twist and Gray transport are **not** NT03–NT09's new results. The old generic μ3 direct-sum proof fails; valid separate witnesses do not repair it. Old H-3A.1/H-3A.2 are not used as premises.

**PC1** is the exploratory Core A principal-hull-preservation conjecture in [03](03_SELECTED_RESEARCH_PROBLEM.md); Arena recommends question-only posture until literature/applicability review. No target depends on PC1 being true.

## 2. Target cards

### NT01 — Source-valid inherited input contract

**Type/status:** E-applicability work, NOT a novelty claim; pending.

**Scope:** r=2, first-slot θ_s/right ideals/central X, beginning t=2,n=2,p=2. Identify exact valid supporting results for E01–E10 and document manuscript proof defects that must not be inherited.

**Required output after authorization:** a lemma-use ledger with source/proof locator, quantifiers, side, slot, scalar field, and unresolved cells. If a valid premise is unavailable, stop or explicitly request separate proof/correction authority. This is not permission to edit the old paper.

**Exit:** no core dependency relies on a known invalid proof, wrong field encoding, stale “open” status or an unreviewed side/form substitution. The claim count is not a proof count.

### NT02 — Faithful local-form and embedding contract

**Type/status:** infrastructure TARGET; originality X, normally background rather than a main theorem.

**Desired statement:** on the retained local model, exhibit an exact dictionary between global principal matrix cyclic inputs, their embedded two-generated Morita modules, and the **induced** local dual/hull condition. For any approved multi-factor extension, identify source/target factors and their twist/reversal transport rather than assume independent local hulls.

**Dependencies:** NT01 and standard CRT; no assumed standard local dot-product form. Existing split/permuted formulas must be acknowledged.

**Proof obligations later:** well-defined domains/codomains, both directions of the dictionary, scalar-field semilinearity, independence from generator presentation, and preservation of the actual hull submodule. Verification of a familiar dictionary is not automatically new mathematics.

**Exit:** a mathematically coherent model against which NT03 can be stated; no full-wild or quantum theorem inferred.

### NT03 — Core generator-profile structure theorem

**Type/status:** principal research TARGET / RQ2–RQ4; truth and external novelty unresolved.

**Desired result:** a structural normal form or finite set of explicitly specified invariants for the **retained embedded principal-input class** that determines μ_T(H_s(W)) and relevant hull layers without enumerating all ambient modules. Begin Core A; extend only if a nonroutine theorem is possible.

**What must be discovered, not assumed:** which minimal-generator relations, radical layers, extension parameters and restricted form/embedding data are sufficient. A basis-independent statement must account for allowed changes of generators and transported ambient forms.

**Proof obligations later:** existence and coverage of the proposed form/class, reconstruction, exact sufficiency of the data, no loss of pairing information, correct zero/boundary cases, and parameter/twist quantifiers. If uniqueness is claimed, it needs its own proof.

**Novelty failure test:** writing μ(H)=dim(H/mH), recomputing a field nullspace, or using an existing MT GPM followed by standard linear algebra is insufficient unless a genuinely additional structural theorem is demonstrated.

**Exit:** a full proof in a future new-research workspace and independent theorem-level comparison with MS03, NL02/NL03/NL10/NL11 and relevant cited algebra. No proof is supplied now.

### NT04 — Principal-input dichotomy / principality criterion

**Type/status:** principal research TARGET / RQ1,RQ3; direction open.

**Desired result:** characterize precisely when μ_T(H_s(W))≤2 for the retained two-generated inputs. Two legitimate branches are:

- **Preservation branch:** establish preservation for a parameter-uniform class, with exact hypotheses and an explanatory reason; assess whether it is nonroutine enough for the paper.
- **Growth branch:** specify an obstruction/criterion yielding μ>2, with a structural family and independently checkable explicit representatives.

**Dependencies:** NT02; preferably NT03 for an explanatory criterion. PC1 is not a dependency. A theorem valid only at q4 by finite census is not the intended universal contribution.

**Exit:** criterion is necessary/sufficient in the stated class or is honestly labelled only one-sided; all quantified claims proved. A counterexample must start from **μ(W)≤2** and have μ(H)>2. A nonprincipal dual or input is not a substitute.

### NT05 — Sharp bounds and attainability within the retained class

**Type/status:** TARGET / RQ5; conditional on a useful NT03/NT04.

**Desired result:** a sharp upper bound, attainable generator spectrum, or preservation classification that explains what hull generator numbers are possible under the selected input/form constraints.

**Proof obligations later:** upper bound over the entire stated class, attainment of every claimed extremum/value or explicit exclusions, and separation of field length, μ, type and matrix-ideal generator number. If no growth occurs, do not manufacture a positive example; characterize the preservation boundary instead.

**Novelty test:** ordinary submodule bounds, standard socle/type dualities and the known matrix packing threshold are compared before any new-bound label. A short specialization of NL11 is not an independent contribution.

**Exit:** sharpness claimed only where supported by a general proof and explicit certificate-ready examples, not a search maximum.

### NT06 — Twist and embedding dependence

**Type/status:** TARGET / RQ4,RQ6; optional co-main result if substantial.

**Desired result:** determine how changing s, or changing embedding data at fixed coarse module type, changes the criterion/profile in the approved class. Include non-reflexive m∤2s only when the exact local/global dual condition is retained.

**Dependencies:** NT02–NT04. Repeating DD=+σ_(2s) or observing hull-dimension variation is prior work, not this result.

**Exit:** any sufficiency/invariance statement has a proof; counterfamilies distinguish genuinely relevant data from a mere coordinate re-encoding. If no new dependence survives, record that rather than invent a twist advantage.

### NT07 — Global synthesis under coupled CRT factors

**Type/status:** conditional extension TARGET / RQ7, not required to start.

**Desired result:** determine whether the local principal-input criterion assembles under actual factor coupling, yielding a meaningful global classification or new obstruction. The component permutation, coefficient-field maps and induced forms must be explicit.

**Dependencies:** NT02–NT05 and independent review of any new generalization. Standard CRT and μ=max(component μ) are background.

**Exit:** nonroutine content beyond reassembling already known component formulas; exact hypotheses on multiplicity, factors and twists. If it is only a routine corollary, identify it as such, not a second main theorem.

### NT08 — Certificate-producing structural computation

**Type/status:** conditional algorithmic TARGET / RQ8; not implemented.

**Desired result:** an algorithm on the retained normal-form/invariant input that outputs the hull generator profile and, when claimed, explicit minimal generators and principality certificates, with a justified cost bound in the stated input model.

**Dependencies:** NT03–NT05 and an independently specified arithmetic/coordinate contract. Proposed implementation/testing is in [06](06_COMPUTATIONAL_METHODOLOGY.md).

**Proof obligations later:** termination on the selected class, correctness, completeness of any enumeration, minimality certificates and a reproducible complexity statement. A finite agreement count cannot prove the algorithm's universal correctness.

**Exit:** proved algorithm claim plus independent checks on named objects; if only a useful reference implementation is achieved, label it supporting computation rather than a theorem-level contribution.

### NT09 — Optional quantum corollary

**Type/status:** optional RQ9 / TARGET, **no bridge established**.

**Desired result:** only if possible, a new structural consequence for an actual Euclidean/Hermitian/trace hull or commutator radical, with a justified metric/physical-label map. Standard EA/CSS formulas are imports; μ(H_s) is not entanglement cost.

**Dependencies:** core algebraic results, all gates in [07](07_QUANTUM_CONSTRUCTION_STRATEGY.md), separate author authority and new-paper audit. Failure does not force abandonment of a sufficiently strong algebraic paper.

**Exit:** complete physical hypotheses, field/trace dimensions, phase/lift or Bob completion, route-correct logical-error set, distance status and matched-resource comparisons. If absent, omit quantum claims rather than weaken these conditions.

## 4. Minimum contribution and claim-promotion rules

Before a new-paper GO recommendation: at least **one nonroutine structural main theorem** plus a meaningful proved consequence/classification must survive independent review and external comparison. Multiple routine lemmas do not meet this by count. Any target may end as: **proved under stated restrictions / refuted with a verified witness / computational evidence only / unresolved / already known / withdrawn**.

For every promoted claim require an exact statement, complete proof locator, reviewed dependencies, explicit range, separate computational evidence, and separate novelty evidence. “All tests PASS” cannot promote TARGET, PC or X to theorem or priority. If only routine translations or old-paper repairs remain, the correct outcome is **STOP/RE-SCOPE**, not a forced Q1 claim.
