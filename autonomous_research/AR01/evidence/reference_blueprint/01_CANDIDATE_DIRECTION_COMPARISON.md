## 4. Recommended primary problem — provisional P2-S

**Working problem:** determine the generator profiles of Galois hulls of principal cyclic codes over M₂(F_q[u]/u²), beginning with a precisely transported repeated-root local model and retaining the embedding data that plain dimensions forget.

The principal-input question is sharper than “nonprincipal hulls exist”: the known μ=3 hulls do not answer it because their input codes are already nonprincipal. The existing1,221-module/2,442-hull audit checks only the specific (q,t,n)=(4,2,2) family; it is not a general theorem over all fields or multiplicities. The [problem specification](03_SELECTED_RESEARCH_PROBLEM.md) treats either a principality-preservation theorem or a structurally classified growth mechanism as a legitimate outcome. No positive answer is assumed.

**Minimum hoped-for advance:** a criterion/bound/classification using input module type **and its pairing/embedding data**, with sharpness or a principality-preservation theorem. Merely computing μ of an already computed hull by Nakayama, adding a finite witness, or renaming a QC hull algorithm is insufficient for the intended paper.

## 5. Disallowed shortcuts and resource comparison

- **P1:** “first q8 EA” is contradicted by included records. DD=+σ_(2s) is inherited. R1 must use its actual generally Fp-linear label group and trace orthogonal.
- **P2:** changing r=2 to r=3 alone is not a research gap. In the general known threshold, principal iff μ_B(W)≤r; at r=3 a nonprincipal example needs μ>3, not merely3. Coefficient-expansion index is tr², hence9t; for q=p²,t=2,r=3 the **base ring** has p^36 elements, and length-n ambient has p^(36n). Those are cost corrections, not new theorems.
- **P3:** field skew+Galois and matrix/chain-ring skew precedents must be compared. Two-sidedness of (X^n−1), the automorphism order, module side, adjunction and shift compatibility cannot be inferred from the central-variable case.
- A fourth direction is **not recommended now**: simultaneous-Galois hull algorithms would face NL09's reported sampling/type work and would require its own gap review. It is not a hidden substitute approved by this blueprint.
