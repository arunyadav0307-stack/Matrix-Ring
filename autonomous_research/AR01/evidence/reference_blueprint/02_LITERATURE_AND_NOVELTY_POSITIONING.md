## 1. Evidence rules

| Label | Meaning here | Permitted inference |
|---|---|---|
| **E — established background** | Standard published mathematics or an inherited audited conditional result, with stated hypotheses | May be a cited input after applicability review; not a new contribution |
| **U — unresolved result** | Existing manuscript/register question or a remaining proof/applicability issue | Not a theorem; old computational evidence stays bounded |
| **PC — proposed conjecture** | Explicitly tentative future proposition, not used as an assumption | May guide later authorized falsification; no present proof claim |
| **RQ — new research question** | The differentiated question proposed for a new project | New *to this proposal*, not certified first in the literature |
| **X — externally unverified** | Priority, exact gap, all-version absence or incompletely checked applicability | Must be qualified; cannot appear as an established novelty claim |

A publisher abstract verifies what a paper **reports**, not its complete proof. An authentic PDF does not validate every ring identification it prints. A dash or no-match search does not prove absence. The old37-entry bibliography and the audited **38 source rows/266 cells** are different sets.

## 4. Exact proposed gap — and what is not yet established

**Internally verified motivation:** MS03's `rem:o31b` and conclusion explicitly leave open whether a principal cyclic code can have a nonprincipal Galois hull in the repeated-root, nilpotent setting. The follow-up's old census confirms no such example in its one checked family; valid existing nonprincipal hulls do not settle the principal-input question.

**RQ:** characterize attainable hull generator profiles, and decide principal-input preservation versus generator growth, first in a restricted rank-two local repeated-root family, keeping the actual induced pairing and embedding information.

**X:** no external source found in this bounded review has been technically shown to settle that exact programme. This sentence is **not** a certification that none does. In particular:

1. A specialization of NL10's scalar classification may settle some cases.
2. NL03/NL04's generators plus the retained nilpotent action may already give the desired criterion by a short standard calculation.
3. NL11 or older commutative algebra may imply a general bound/preservation theorem.
4. A nonprincipal hull of a principal input might already be a known module-intersection example under another terminology.

Each possibility is a Phase1/2 stop-or-narrow test. Simply adding the word “Morita” or changing matrix size does not differentiate a result.

## 5. Theorem-level comparison protocol for the first authorized phase

For **every** proposed core target in [05](05_THEOREM_ROADMAP.md), create a comparison row with:

- exact statement, quantifiers, field/ring, side, slot and twist convention;
- whether inputs are arbitrary modules, ideals, scalar cyclic codes or **principal matrix cyclic codes**;
- whether the output is field dimension, a generator matrix, **minimal B-generator number**, or a structural characterization;
- induced reversal/component permutation and nilpotent action;
- proof mechanism and whether the proposed result follows by a routine specialization;
- primary theorem/page/version locator, access level and unresolved applicability cells;
- separate assessments of **truth**, **printed proof**, **finite evidence**, **newness** and **priority**.

Required positive comparisons include the original MS03, foundational anti-isomorphism work, NL01–NL06, NL10–NL11, and any predecessor they cite that supplies the crucial μ/intersection mechanism. Quantum or P3 expansions additionally require NL07–NL09 and the retained matrix/Gray/EA predecessors. Abstract-only review is insufficient for an affirmative gap verdict. If a needed primary text cannot be obtained, retain X and postpone the dependent claim; do not simulate access.

## 6. Novelty language permitted now

**Permitted:** “We propose to investigate generator profiles of Galois hulls of principal matrix-ring cyclic codes, an internal open question requiring further external comparison.”

**Not permitted:** “first”, “the literature has no such result”, “Q1-ready”, “low novelty risk”, “new merely in origin”, “all predecessors lack Gray/quantum constructions”, or “a true corrected old bound is a new theorem”.

The new paper must cite and delimit MS03 honestly even if the old manuscript is unpublished or on HOLD. Actual publication/submission lineage is an author fact (NAD02); the blueprint cannot choose how much prior work to disclose by renaming it. No new manuscript text is drafted here.
